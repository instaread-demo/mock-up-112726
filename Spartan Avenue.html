3 bold predictions for Michigan State’s Thanksgiving Day showdown with Arkansas

Michigan State will look to feast on some Razorbacks.

The next “big” game on the schedule for Michigan State basketball is here and the Spartans are staying home this Thanksgiving for a showdown with Arkansas at Little Caesars Arena.

Michigan State is 5-1 on the year and has dropped from No. 2 to No. 11 in the rankings after losing to Tennessee by 15 last week and Arkansas is 4-2 with losses to Virginia and Indiana. The Razorbacks have dropped from the rankings, but beating John Calipari is never an easy task.

Thursday’s matchup between the Spartans and Razorbacks is going to be electric. What better way to cap off Thanksgiving?

Let’s dive into three of my boldest predictions of the season for Thursday’s game.

Jeremy Fears Jr. flirts with a triple-double

Jeremy Fears Jr. has never recorded a triple-double in his Michigan State career but he’s going to flirt with his first on Thursday evening. Although he’s going to finish a couple of rebounds shy of a triple-double, he’ll still record 17 points, 11 assists, and eight boards. He’ll add three steals, really shining in the spotlight as he tries to get Michigan State back on track.

The All-American point guard needs to play his “A” game if the Spartans are going to beat a pesky Arkansas team that may have fallen out of the rankings but it’s still one of the more talented teams in college basketball.

Fears will have a true national player of the year-caliber performance.

Ethan Taylor has his first big game

The center position has been a hot topic this season and that’s not changing any time soon but it’ll see a positive development on Thursday evening at LCA as Ethan Taylor has his first big game as a Spartan.

The freshman center will finish with 10-plus points for the first time in his Michigan State career and he’ll grab seven boards with two blocks. He’s going to take some pressure off Tom Izzo who’s trying to figure out who his starter is going to be. Taylor will have a career game against the Razorbacks when the Spartans need him most.

Taylor is also going to play the most minutes at center and he’ll finally use that massive 7-foot-2 frame to his advantage. Cam Ward had a breakout freshman game last year against Arkansas and now it’s Taylor’s turn.

Michigan State scores 90

The last time Michigan State had a “big” game against an opponent that began the season ranked, the Spartans scored 46 and lost a clunker. This time around, they’ll score 90 in a winning effort.

Izzo isn’t going to allow his team to show up ill-prepared this time and there’s going to be no 9-for-50 shooting performance at LCA. Arkansas is a talented, athletic team so scoring 90 on the Razorbacks won’t be an easy task, but Virginia already did and Indiana dropped 81. Michigan State’s offense is capable of big things and we’ll see flashes of greatness.

Michigan State drops 90 points and covers the spread against an Arkansas team that’s going to inevitably contend for an SEC title. Michigan State wins 91-81.
